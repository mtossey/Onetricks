Definitions

  • Each sampled player's window is their newest 30 ranked solo games, remakes removed. Players need at least 15 games to be classified.
  • Main: 30% or more of the window on the champion. Includes OTPs.
  • OTP (one-trick): 70% or more of the window on one champion.
  • Roles: a main counts in their most-played role on the champion, using only those games. Their off-role games (mostly autofill) count in "All roles". A role is listed for a champion once it has 30 games.

Why are mains' win rates above 50%?

Players were taken from the current Master+ ladder, so they recently won more than average, mostly on their mains. A high mains win rate shows how these players do, not that the champion is strong.

"All players WR" is every player on the champion in the same matches, so it shows how the champion itself is doing.

Reading the numbers

Intervals are Wilson 95% intervals on games. Games from the same player are related, so the real uncertainty is somewhat larger, especially for champions with few mains.
